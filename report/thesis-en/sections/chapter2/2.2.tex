The comparison leads to the following observations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Supporting several forms of description has become the norm.} All three systems support search by attributes and by an image or appearance sample, but within the surveyed documentation only Verkada supports free-text search, a function that appeared in 2024. Verkada's approach, encoding images and query sentences into one representation space and searching a vector database, lets one mechanism serve both forms.
    \item \textbf{Search usually comes with tools for compiling results}, such as Avigilon's bookmarking and evidence export: users also need to store and share results during an investigation.
    \item \textbf{Commercial systems are tied to the vendor's infrastructure}: BriefCam needs a server with a dedicated graphics card, Avigilon needs the vendor's software and devices, and Verkada runs on the vendor's cloud. Cost and vendor lock-in are barriers for small organisations or those that must keep data on premises. These products also bundle many functions beyond search, such as alerts, face recognition, licence plates and statistics.
\end{itemize}
