\section{Use Case Specifications}
\label{sec:4.5}

This section specifies the eight use cases of the main workflow: a user logs in (UC-01); the Administrator adds a camera (UC-03), puts it under analysis (UC-04) and chooses the models (UC-05); the Operator searches (UC-09), assesses the results (UC-10) and saves one into a case (UC-11); the Viewer reads the cases (UC-13). Exceptions are marked E and alternative flows A. The other use cases are covered by the requirements of Section~\ref{sec:4.2}.

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-01: Log in}\label{tab:uc01}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Administrator, Operator, Viewer \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Access the functions and data of the user's role. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The user has an active account. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The system creates a login session and takes the user to the home page of the role. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc01})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The user opens the login page and enters a username and password. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}The system checks the credentials and the status of the account. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The system creates a login session and determines the role of the account. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}The system opens the home page of the role, showing only its functions. \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Wrong credentials:} The same failure message is shown for a wrong username or password, and the attempt is logged. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - Locked or deactivated account:} The system refuses the login. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E3 - Too many attempts:} The system refuses temporarily and states how long to wait. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & None. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-03: Manage cameras}\label{tab:uc03}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Administrator \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Add and update cameras, check their RTSP connection, retire them and bring them back. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Administrator is logged in; the list of surveillance areas has been declared. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The camera's information, status and RTSP check result are saved. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc03})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The Administrator opens camera management; the system lists the cameras with their area, operational, RTSP and AI processing status. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}The Administrator adds a camera with its code, name, area and RTSP address, optionally with credentials; a camera processed only through video files needs no address. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The system validates the data, stores the credentials encrypted and apart from the address, and saves the camera as \emph{Unverified}. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}The system connects to the RTSP stream and updates the connection status. \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Invalid data:} Missing information, an invalid RTSP address or an existing camera code is refused. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - Connection fails:} The camera is saved as \emph{Offline} with a warning, to be fixed and checked again. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E3 - Address outside the permitted network:} An address outside the camera network ranges declared at deployment is not checked; the camera stays \emph{Unverified}. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & \textbf{A1 - Update a camera:} The name or RTSP address can be edited, not the area; a new address is checked again from \emph{Unverified}. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{A2 - Check the connection again:} The Administrator requests a new connection check. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{A3 - Retire from operation:} AI processing is disabled and the running job cancelled; existing data is kept but cannot be searched. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{A4 - Bring back into operation:} The camera returns with AI processing disabled, and its data can be searched again. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-04: Manage AI processing of cameras}\label{tab:uc04}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Administrator \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Choose which cameras are analysed, and process a recorded video file for a camera when needed. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Administrator is logged in; the camera exists and is operational. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The camera's AI processing status is updated, and the camera enters or leaves the analysis. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc04})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The Administrator opens AI processing management; the system lists the cameras with their AI processing status and the model configuration in use. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}The Administrator enables or disables AI processing for a camera and confirms. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The system checks the status of the camera and the current model configuration. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}The system saves the status. An enabled camera is analysed in turn with the others; a disabled camera's running job is cancelled and no new data is created, while existing data is kept. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{5.}The system reports the result to the Administrator. \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Camera offline:} AI processing cannot be enabled for an RTSP camera that has lost its connection. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - AI models not ready:} AI processing cannot be enabled while the model configuration is incomplete or unavailable. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E3 - Camera no longer operational:} The AI processing status of a retired camera cannot change. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E4 - Invalid video file:} A file with a wrong format or size is refused and no job is created. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & \textbf{A1 - Process a video file:} The Administrator uploads a recorded video for a camera with its start time and sampling mode. The system checks the file and creates a job that uses the same pipeline as an RTSP stream, showing its progress. This is the only way to process a camera without RTSP. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-05: Configure AI models}\label{tab:uc05}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Administrator \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Choose the Detector and Tracker of the whole system; the encoders are fixed. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Administrator is logged in; the system has a registered list of Detectors and Trackers. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The chosen pair becomes the current configuration for new jobs. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc05})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The Administrator opens the model configuration; the system shows the pair in use and the registered models, with unavailable ones marked and disabled. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}The Administrator chooses a Detector, a Tracker or both, then confirms. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The system checks that the models are ready and compatible, then trial-loads them. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}The system saves the new configuration and reports the result. \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Model unavailable:} The system refuses to apply it and keeps the previous valid configuration. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - Incompatible Detector and Tracker:} The Administrator is asked to choose another pair. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & None. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-09: Search for people with AI}\label{tab:uc09}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Operator \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Find a person in the analysed data of the assigned area by image, English description or attributes. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Operator is logged in; the account's area has analysed data. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & At most $k$ results within the scope are shown by decreasing relevance. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc09})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The Operator gives an image, an English description or attributes. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}The Operator may choose cameras of the area, a time range and $k$ among 4, 8, 12 and 16, then sends the request. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The system validates the query and limits the scope to the operational cameras of the area. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}The system encodes the query and takes the $k$ most relevant indexed results. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{5.}The system shows each result's person image, camera, area, time and relevance score. \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Invalid query:} A wrong image format, a non-English description, invalid attributes or a disallowed $k$ is reported. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - No data in scope:} The system suggests widening the time range or adding cameras. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E3 - Search components unavailable:} If the encoder or vector store is down, the search fails explicitly instead of returning an empty list. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E4 - Request out of scope:} A direct request for a camera outside the area is refused. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & \textbf{A1 - Search again from a result:} The Operator drags a result's image into the search box to search for the same person. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-10: View and assess search results}\label{tab:uc10}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Operator \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Decide which results really show the person being sought. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Operator has performed UC-09 and the system has returned at least one result. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The results are assessed; no data changes. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc10})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The Operator views the list of results. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}Selecting a result shows its full frame with the person's bounding box and details. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The Operator compares it with the person sought, then other results. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}To keep a result, the Operator chooses \textbf{Create new case} or \textbf{Add to existing case} (UC-11). \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Result no longer available:} A removed result is reported as no longer available. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - Image cannot be shown:} The other information is still shown. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & \textbf{A1 - Sort results:} The Operator sorts the list by relevance or by time. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{A2 - Filter results:} The Operator filters the list by camera or time range, without a new query. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-11: Manage case files}\label{tab:uc11}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Operator \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Save the results of an incident into an own case and follow it until closed. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Operator is logged in; the result to save is within the Operator's search scope. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & The case is created or updated, with one owner, a title, notes, a status and saved results. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc11})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}From a search result, the Operator chooses \textbf{Create new case} or \textbf{Add to existing case}. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}A new case needs a title and notes, and its owner is the logged-in account; for an existing case, only the Operator's open cases are listed. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The result is saved as a new entry, even if the appearance is already in the case. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}The Operator may edit the title and notes and remove results; the appearance itself is kept. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{5.}When done, the Operator marks the case \textbf{Closed}; the system records the time and locks it. \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - Result or case no longer available:} Nothing is saved and the cases are reloaded. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - Result outside the Operator's rights:} A direct request to save a result outside the scope is refused. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E3 - Case already closed:} No change is allowed until the case is reopened. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E4 - Data changed elsewhere:} The update is refused and the case must be reloaded. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & \textbf{A1 - Close while saving:} With \textbf{Mark the case as closed} ticked, the case is closed after the save; if closing fails, the result stays saved and the Operator is asked to close it again. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{A2 - Reopen a case:} A closed case returns to \textbf{Open} and can be edited. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}|p{12.3cm}|}
\caption{Use case specification UC-13: View case files}\label{tab:uc13}\\
\hline
\textbf{Actor} & Viewer \\ \hline
\textbf{Purpose} & Read every case and its saved results. \\ \hline
\textbf{Precondition} & The Viewer is logged in. \\ \hline
\textbf{Postcondition} & No data changes. \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{(continued from Table~\ref{tab:uc13})}}\\
\hline
\endhead
\textbf{Main flow} & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{1.}The Viewer opens the case files; the system lists every case. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{2.}The Viewer may filter by status, creation time or Operator, then selects a case. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{3.}The system shows the title, notes, status, Operator, creation time and saved results, without relevance scores. \\
 & \hangindent=1.6em\hangafter=1\noindent\makebox[1.6em][l]{4.}Selecting a result shows its details (UC-14). \\ \hline
\textbf{Exceptions} & \textbf{E1 - No cases yet:} The system shows an empty state. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E2 - Case cannot be retrieved:} The error is reported and the Viewer can retry. \\ \cline{2-2}
 & \textbf{E3 - Some results no longer available:} The case is shown with the missing results marked. \\ \hline
\textbf{Alternative flows} & \textbf{A1 - Open from the overview:} A case selected in the recent-cases list of UC-12 opens directly. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}
