\section{Objectives}
\label{sec:1.2}

The overall objective is an application that helps users search for people in surveillance camera data by describing them in several ways instead of reviewing video by hand. The system proposes and ranks results; the decision whether a result is the person sought remains with the user.

The specific objectives are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Turn camera data into searchable data.} Automatically analyse the camera images, detect and track people, and store each appearance of a person as a searchable unit.

    \item \textbf{Search for people with multimodal descriptions.} Describe the person with a reference image, a sentence or predefined appearance features, all matched against the analysed data and ranked by relevance.

    \item \textbf{Support the assessment and management of search results.} Review each result in its original context and save confirmed results into case files for follow-up.

    \item \textbf{Operate safely with many users.} Grant permissions by responsibility, limit each person's searches to their assigned area, and provide administration and monitoring functions.

    \item \textbf{Assess the applicability of existing artificial intelligence models.} Without training new models, select published models suited to the problem and to commodity hardware, and evaluate their search quality and processing cost in the application.

    % [MERGE] Part II objective.
    \item \textbf{Study how to improve search from text descriptions.} Separately from the application, the project investigates a reasoning layer that reads human-readable attribute evidence about each candidate and re-ranks the results of text-based search, and measures what it gains and what it costs against a conventional retrieval baseline.
\end{enumerate}

The project is considered to have met its objectives when the application runs the whole workflow, from receiving camera data, searching and assessing results to saving case files, with measurements of search quality and performance presented in Chapter~\ref{chapter:kiemthu}, and when the reasoning layer of objective 6 has been evaluated as reported in Chapter~\ref{chapter:r-experiments}.
