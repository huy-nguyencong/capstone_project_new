WHAT MUST NOT BE CUT
--------------------
This is the COARSE round. Everything you place below the cut is gone and no
later stage can bring it back, while a candidate you merely rank low can still
be recovered by the round after this one. The two mistakes are not symmetric.

PRESENT means read ANYWHERE, exactly OR as a [near] value standing in for it:
in one frame, as a minority reading, overshadowed by a competing value, or in
the track but not in this crop. NEAR MATCH ONLY is present. A candidate reading
a value either of those ways outranks a candidate whose notes say that value is
read NOWHERE, when the table above calls it decisive. Present but weak beats
absent.

ABSENT is the EVIDENCE GAP standing, in either of the two forms the notes use:
`read nowhere in the track` - silence - and `nothing closer than [far] read
anywhere` - contradiction. Neither is weakness, and a [far] reading is NOT a
weak match for this floor to protect: it is a mismatch, and WHAT MAKES A READING
WEIGH still sinks it.

This is NOT counting how many attributes match, and it is not a score. It bites
in one place only: an ABSENCE, of a value the table calls decisive. An absence
of a value the table calls almost nothing is not a reason for anything, and two
candidates that both reach a value - exactly or near - are ordered by the
evidence as usual, never by which of them reads it in more frames.
